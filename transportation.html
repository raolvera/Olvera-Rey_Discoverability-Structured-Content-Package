Transportation & Parking

Last updated: August 28, 2026 | prices and lot availability change per event; confirm before you drive.

Getting to Mountain America Stadium in Tempe is easy whether you drive or ride the Valley Metro light rail. Compare parking lots or skip the traffic and hop off the train steps from campus.

Parking Lots

Gameday parking options near campus (typical, per-event)
Lot / structure Approx. distance Typical price Notes

Packard Drive Lot 10-min walk $15–$25 Popular tailgate lot; opens hours before kickoff.

Valley Metro Light Rail

The light rail drops you within a short walk of the stadium and avoids parking altogether. These stops are closest to campus.

Nearest light rail stops to Mountain America Stadium
Station Line Walk to stadium Notes
Veterans Way / College Ave Valley Metro Rail 8 min Closest stop; busiest on gameday.
University Dr / Rural Rd Valley Metro Rail 12 min Good alternate to avoid crowds.
Dorsey / Apache Blvd Valley Metro Rail 15 min Near east-side parking garages.
Tempe Transportation Center Bus + Rail hub 10 min Connects regional bus routes to the rail line.

Getting-There Tips

Buy Transit Passes Ahead

Load a Valley Metro pass on the app before you go so you're not stuck at a ticket machine in a gameday crowd.

Rideshare Drop-Off Zones

Designated rideshare zones sit a few blocks out. Walking a bit past the congestion is usually faster than a curbside pickup.

Leave Buffer Time

Traffic and screening lines build fast. Plan to arrive at least an hour before kickoff for a relaxed entry.
