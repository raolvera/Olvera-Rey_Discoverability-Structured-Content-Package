FAQ & Contact

Quick answers to the questions we hear most, plus a form to send us a note about anything gameday related.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are tickets digital or paper?

Tickets are almost always mobile/digital. Add them to your phone wallet and take a screenshot as a backup before you leave home.

Can I leave and come back in?

Most games are no re-entry. Once you exit, you generally can't be readmitted on the same ticket, so plan your trips out accordingly.

What should I do about the heat?

Early-season Tempe games can be very hot. Wear light colors, use sunscreen, and take advantage of shaded concourses and water stations inside.

Is the stadium family and kid friendly?

Yes. Children who occupy a seat need a ticket. Remember diaper bags must follow the clear-bag policy on the Stadium Guide page.

Where do I find accessible parking and seating?

Accessible (ADA) parking is available with a valid placard or plate; arrive early. See the Transportation & Parking page for lots and light rail options.
