Stadium Guide & Policies

Last updated: August 28, 2026 | always confirm on official sources before gameday.

Mountain America Stadium enforces a clear-bag policy and security screening at every gate. Review what you can bring, what's prohibited, and when gates open so you clear security fast.

Clear-Bag Policy

Permitted and prohibited bags at Mountain America Stadium
Bag type Allowed? Notes
Clear plastic, vinyl, or PVC tote Yes Must not exceed 12" x 6" x 12".
One-gallon clear resealable bag Yes Standard storage-size freezer bag is fine.
Small clutch or wristlet Yes Roughly 4.5" x 6.5", with or without a strap. One per guest.
Backpacks and large purses No Non-clear backpacks, totes, and oversized bags are not permitted.
Diaper bags No Carry supplies in a permitted clear bag instead.

Prohibited Items

Common items not allowed inside the stadium
Item Status Notes
Outside food & beverages Prohibited Sealed water may be restricted; check current policy.
Umbrellas Prohibited Even collapsible umbrellas are typically not allowed.
Selfie sticks & tripods Prohibited Professional camera gear needs credentials.
Noisemakers & laser pointers Prohibited Air horns and artificial noisemakers are banned.
Weapons of any kind Prohibited No firearms, knives, or pepper spray, regardless of permits.

Gate Times & Screening

When do gates open?
Gates generally open 90 minutes before kickoff. Arrive early for high-demand games to clear screening comfortably.
What does screening involve?
All guests pass through walk-through metal detectors. Have your clear bag open and phone or keys ready to speed the line.
Is there re-entry?
Most games are no re-entry. Once you leave, you may not be readmitted on the same ticket.
Mobile tickets
Tickets are typically digital. Download and screenshot yours in advance in case cell service is slow near the gates.
